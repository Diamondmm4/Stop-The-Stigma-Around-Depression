What We Learned

Symptoms of depression include:

  1. Loss of motivation
  2. Loss of enjoyment in activities you once enjoyed
  3. Overall long period of loss of morale
  4. Dropping grades (for students)
  5. Negative self thoughts

Ways you can find assistance:

  1. Journaling/self-reflection
  2. Talking to a close friend
  3. Talking with your parents
  4. Receiving therapy and talking with a mental health professional
  5. Using the 988 Suicide and Crisis Lifeline

What We Learned

As a friend of someone experiencing depression, you have a lot of power to influence their mental health. Your actions can help or hurt them, so watch what you say and how you interact with them

Depending on the severity of their depression, you can:

  • Talk to them if they want to
  • Help them if they sincerely ask for help
  • Support them; don’t judge
  • Speak with their parents or siblings so they can be taken proper care of at home

What We Learned

As the sibling of someone facing mental health, you are able to get them real, professional help easily if you do the right thing

The best first step is to talk to them since they spend a lot of their time around you and may have known and lived with you for most of your lives

To make sure they receive the proper support and care they need, you can help by:

  • Speaking with your parents
  • Connecting them with a mental health professional/therapist if possible
  • Offering to be available if they need someone to speak with or spend time with
  • Listening to them attentively whenever they need to talk to you

In summary, be there for them whenever and if ever they need anything!

What We Learned

Being simply a distant classmate to someone experiencing depression is a unique position to go about helping them from. This is because you're not close enough to the person to comfortably talk to them about their mental health in most cases. But there is always a way to help them

As a student, you should always recognize that your teachers are not only as close as they are to you than everyone else, but they can lead to many more people like the parents of other students

When another student's mental health difficulties are serious enough to be recognized in school, it’s always best to tell a teacher because they can talk to not only the student, but also their parents
